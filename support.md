# Neyvu Companion support

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Contact: **jpss2004@gmail.com**

Neyvu Companion connects your own Acadia Moodle session to your own Neyvu vault. The current release is an invitation-only beta.

## Before you start

You need Windows 11 (x64), Microsoft Edge, an internet connection, an invited Google account and your own Acadia Moodle account. The web app is at [Neyvu beta](https://schoolvault-beta-web.onrender.com/beta). Desktop downloads are currently held. The Neyvu Windows package has passed package validation in the Store draft; fresh certification, Store signing and a protected installation check remain required. Mac downloaded installation is also being qualified. The first guided cohort will start when both desktop Companions are ready; a reserved Store link is not an available download.

## Connect your courses

1. Install Neyvu Companion from its approved Microsoft Store listing when available.
2. Open the companion and choose to connect your Neyvu account. Sign in with your invited Google account in the browser and confirm this laptop.
3. Open Moodle from the companion and sign in directly in its separate Edge window.
4. Confirm the displayed Moodle identity and review your current lecture and separate lab selections before importing. If a current lab is missing from In progress, check All and select it explicitly.
5. Return to Neyvu on your laptop or phone to use your own courses, notes and chat.

Keep the companion running and the laptop awake for collection. If a Moodle session expires, sign in again in its separate window. You do not need to forward email or connect Notion to begin this beta.

## What the beta collects

The companion collects supported visible course and activity text and can acquire supported linked files. Review collection and document reading separately: a saved original may still be waiting to be read or may be unreadable. Named gaps identify encountered problems, and recoverable file reads can be retried. Restricted or unloaded content, unsupported files, external media, grades and submission receipts may remain unavailable. A connection is not proof that every source was collected or understood. Compare important details with Moodle and your originals.

## Disconnect or request help

Revoke a laptop in the Neyvu web app to block further uploads from its connection. **Disconnect this laptop** clears local pairing and the isolated Moodle browser sign-in after confirming disconnection. If that confirmation fails, retry with internet access or revoke the laptop on the website first. Closing or uninstalling the companion does not delete the cloud vault. Contact support for account deletion or safe local-data removal.

Send the exact error message, operating system, Companion version and step where it happened. When signed in, you can explicitly share a pilot feedback report from the workspace; private notes and chats are not attached automatically. Do not send passwords, verification codes, connection codes, cookies or private course files unless a specific secure support method has been agreed.
