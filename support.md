# SchoolVault Companion support

Contact: **jpss2004@gmail.com**

SchoolVault Companion connects your own Acadia Moodle session to your own SchoolVault vault. The current release is an invitation-only beta.

## Before you start

You need Windows 11 (x64), Microsoft Edge, an internet connection, an invited Google account and your own Acadia Moodle account. The web app is at [SchoolVault beta](https://schoolvault-beta-web.onrender.com/beta). The Microsoft Store release is still awaiting certification; a reserved Store link is not yet an available download.

## Connect your courses

1. Install SchoolVault Companion from its approved Microsoft Store listing when available.
2. Open the companion and choose to connect your SchoolVault account. Sign in with your invited Google account in the browser and confirm this laptop.
3. Open Moodle from the companion and sign in directly in its separate Edge window.
4. Check and confirm the displayed Moodle identity before collecting course material.
5. Return to SchoolVault on your laptop or phone to use your own courses, notes and chat.

Keep the companion running and the laptop awake for collection. If a Moodle session expires, sign in again in its separate window. You do not need to forward email or connect Notion to begin this beta.

## What the beta collects

The companion reads visible text on supported Moodle course and activity pages. It reports missing coverage. Its current capture does not automatically collect PDFs, external videos, grades or deeper forum/book pages. A connection is not a guarantee that every course source has been read.

## Disconnect or request help

Revoke a laptop in the SchoolVault web app to block further uploads from its connection. Reset removes the companion's local pairing, but retains its separate Moodle browser profile. Closing or uninstalling the companion does not delete the cloud vault. Contact support for account deletion or safe local-data removal.

Send the exact error message, Windows version and step where it happened. Do not send passwords, verification codes, connection codes, cookies or private course files unless a specific secure support method has been agreed.
